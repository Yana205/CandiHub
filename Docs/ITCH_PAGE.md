## Page body

### The story

Your grandmother ran the sweetest little bakery in the village — the kind of
place where the bears lined up before the door even opened. When she finally
hung up her apron, she left you the shop, the counter, the big glass display
case... and her famous recipe book.

Except the book is almost empty. The pages are gone — and the display case
holds only dark, sugar-dusted silhouettes of the desserts she used to make.

Grandma never wrote her recipes down. She said you'd find them the way she
did: **by baking.**

Merge desserts on the counter and her recipes come back to you, one by one —
each new creation lights up its place in the glass case, just like it stood in
her day. The bears remember, too. They keep coming in and asking for their old
favorites, even the ones you haven't rediscovered yet.

Fill the case. Feed the bears. Bring grandma's menu back.

### How to play

- **Tap** to drop a dessert into the box on the counter.
- **Two identical desserts** that touch merge into the next one in the chain:
  Mochi → Purin → Melon Pan → Choco Donut → Roll Cake.
- After the donut, desserts come in **colors** — only same-color desserts merge.
- When a bear orders, **press & hold** the matching dessert to send it flying
  into their paws. Serving pays coins and frees space in the box.
- **Shake** (charged by merging) settles the pile; **Day-old clearance**
  (costs coins) pops the little ones when things get dangerous.
- The run ends when the pile overflows the box. Sold out!

### Tips from grandma

- A customer can ask for something you haven't baked yet — that's not a
  mistake, it's a goal. Go build it.
- Selling is the only way to shrink the pile. Don't hoard the pretty ones.
- The silhouettes in the case count up (1/3, 2/3...) toward the next recipe —
  peek at them when you're deciding what to merge.
